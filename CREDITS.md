# Credits

**Grim Mouse** by Tobias Pfaff (2014–2016), the point-and-click mod for Grim
Fandango in ResidualVM: https://github.com/tobiaspfaff/grim_mouse
(GPL-2.0-or-later). This project starts from it: its engine code (ported to
ScummVM 2026.4), its hand-drawn hotspots, its patches to the game's scripts,
its cursors and inventory images, and its `mouse.lua` (the source here was
recovered from the shipped bytecode with scummvm-tools' `delua`; the original
source was never published).

**ScummVM** (https://www.scummvm.org, GPL-2.0-or-later), whose Grim engine,
ResidualVM before it, makes Grim Fandango playable at all. Its
`grim-patch.lab` fixes to the game's scripts are merged into ours.

**Grim Fandango** is LucasArts' (1998), now Double Fine's. None of the game's
own files are here: you need your own copy.
